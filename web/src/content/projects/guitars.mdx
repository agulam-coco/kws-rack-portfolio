I started playing guitar during COVID, like a lot of people did. When I got to Grinnell in 2024 I picked up bass, and these days that's mostly what I play. I still love guitar though.

![Playing bass under blue lights](/media/projects/guitars/hero.webp)

I'm more into improv than learning songs note for note. I just put something on and try to play along with whatever comes up 🎸

## What I play

My main guitar right now is a pink Charvel I picked up in Chicago. I play it pretty much every day.

Before that I had a PRS, which I completely sticker bombed. No regrets.
