Freesound is Grinnell's student music group, and I'm one of the managers. It's been around since 1998, when two students started it because they wanted a music scene on campus that was more open to everybody ([Freesound](https://freesound.sites.grinnell.edu/about-freesound/)).

## The practice room

We also have a practice room in the basement of Main, in Gardner Lounge. Students can get access to the gear in there and use it to get ready for a show, get better, or just jam out with their friends. Late night, chill time, whatever. It's a cool place to be.

## Events

In spring we teamed up with Weekend, the student events committee, and put on the first ever Battle of the Bands. It was a full house. I helped coordinate it and played in it with my band ([S&B](https://thesandb.com/55369/arts/freesound-and-weekend-host-first-battle-of-the-bands/)). We want to make it a yearly thing.

## The website

I set up the [Freesound website](https://freesound.us.reclaim.cloud/). It's a React and Vite site with a small Express server, hosted on Reclaim Cloud.

My favorite part was the managers page. I made AI spinning action figures of all the managers, so each of us is a little toy that spins on the page. I had way too much fun making those.

## Meeting one of the founders

I also got to meet one of the original founders of Freesound. He coached me a bit and told me his vision for it. He never expected it to last this long, and it's still going almost 30 years later.

## Making it last past me

Right now, if a manager wants something changed on the website, it has to go through me. That's not going to work once I graduate.

So I'm building a pipeline where future managers can just talk to an AI bot, tell it what they want changed, like "add this event" or "swap my photo," and it makes the change to the site for them. That way nobody has to know how to code to keep it updated, and the site can carry on even after I'm gone, the same way Freesound carried on after the founders left.
